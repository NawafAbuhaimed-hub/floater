Floater — a task tracker that floats above every window
======================================================

INSTALL
  1. Drag Floater into the Applications folder shown here.
  2. Open it from Applications.

FIRST LAUNCH — macOS WILL BLOCK IT
  Floater is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate, so macOS
  refuses to open it the first time and may say it is "damaged". It is not.
  It just has no certificate Apple recognises.

  To open it:
    - Try to open Floater once, and let it be blocked.
    - Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
    - Scroll down. There will be a line about Floater being blocked,
      with an "Open Anyway" button. Click it, then confirm.

  You only have to do this once.

  If macOS still refuses, open Terminal and run:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Floater.app

USING IT
  Floater has no Dock icon. It lives in the menu bar — look for the timer
  glyph. That is where you show or hide the floating pill, change settings,
  and quit.

  - Click the chevron on the pill to open the task list.
  - Type a task, press Return.
  - Hover a task and click 15, 30 or 45 to start a focus timer.
  - Click the circle to cycle its status; the check button marks it done.
  - Drag the pill anywhere. Drag the bottom-right corner to resize.

WHAT IT ASKS FOR
  Calendar / Reminders — only if you use follow-ups or the completion log.
  Both are optional and the app works fully without them.

  Notifications — for the "time's up" banner.

  Chat tab — needs your OWN Anthropic API key from console.anthropic.com.
  It is stored in your Mac's Keychain and sent only to Anthropic. You pay
  for your own usage, a fraction of a cent per message. Everything else in
  Floater works without a key.

REQUIREMENTS
  macOS 14 or later. Runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel.

YOUR DATA
  Tasks, notes and history are stored locally on your Mac and never leave it.
